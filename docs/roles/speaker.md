# Speaker

The speaker is the person Speech Gym exists for. They bring a speech they have prepared, deliver it to the room, and leave with feedback and numbers they can use on the next attempt. The room may be full of friends, full of AI, or a mix.

**Filled by:** a human. This is the one role AI never takes.

## What the speaker does

- **Sets up the session.** Enters a speech title and a target length, and runs a quick mic check. In rooms with video, the check also previews the camera and lighting.
- **Tells the coach what to listen for.** Before speaking, the speaker picks one to three goals for this attempt, such as "fewer filler words" or "a stronger opening". The coach, human or AI, measures the speech against those goals.
- **Delivers the speech.** The speaker talks while the app records and transcribes. There is no pausing or editing, the same as speaking to a live audience.
- **Reviews the feedback.** Afterward, the speaker reads or listens to the evaluation and the delivery reports.
- **Comes back and tries again.** The speaker can repeat the same speech in a later session and compare the results with earlier attempts.

## What the speaker practices

- **Structure.** Building a speech with a clear opening, body, and close that a listener can follow on first hearing.
- **Storytelling and persuasion.** Making a point through stories and examples, not only statements.
- **Timing and pace.** Landing inside the target length and speaking at a speed that is easy to follow, checked against measured numbers.
- **Self-awareness.** Seeing habits that are hard to notice from the inside, such as filler words, rushed sections, and repeated phrases, in the transcript and reports.
- **Using feedback.** Turning an evaluation into one or two concrete changes, then testing them in the next attempt.
- **Confidence.** Getting comfortable through repetition, at any hour, without needing to gather an audience first.

## What the speaker provides before speaking

- Speech title
- Target length, for example 5 to 7 minutes
- One to three goals for this attempt, passed to the coach
- Optional: a short introduction for the Captain to read

## What the speaker gets afterward

- The coach's feedback, measured against the stated goals
- The Pacer's report: actual length against the target
- The Spotter's report: filler words and how often they occurred
- The Form Checker's comments on word choice
- The transcript of the speech
- A comparison with earlier attempts at the same speech, when there are any
